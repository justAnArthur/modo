import SidebarApp from './index'

# Preset

The preset as it ships: collapsed. Press the trigger (or `[`) and the sidebar slides in; pick a thread and the active row melts over to it.

<div className="h-[560px] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background">
  <SidebarApp contained />
</div>

# Open

`defaultOpen` starts it expanded. Open the workspace switcher, a thread's "More options" or the user row: each menu grows out of its row.

<div className="h-[560px] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background">
  <SidebarApp contained defaultOpen />
</div>

# Open on hover

`openOnHover`: move to the left edge or onto the topbar's trigger and the collapsed sidebar opens; leave it and it closes. Press the trigger to keep it open, again to go back to on hover.

<div className="h-[560px] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background">
  <SidebarApp contained openOnHover />
</div>

# Floating on the right

The same shell with `variant="floating"` and `side="right"`; the shortcut becomes `]`.

<div className="h-[560px] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background">
  <SidebarApp contained defaultOpen variant="floating" side="right" />
</div>
