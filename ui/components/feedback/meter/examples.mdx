# Bar

A label, the share on the right and a bar under both.

# Thresholds

From `warnAt` (0.75) the fill turns to the warning hue, from `errorAt` (0.9) to the error hue. Both move.

<div className="flex w-72 flex-col gap-4">
  <Meter label="Strict budget" value={0.55} warnAt={0.5} errorAt={0.7} />
  <Meter label="Out of tokens" value={180000} max={200000} detail="180k / 200k" />
</div>

# Pace

A `marker` ticks where an even spend would be by now; a caller that judges the pace itself passes `tone`.

<div className="flex w-72 flex-col gap-4">
  <Meter label="Weekly" value={0.62} marker={0.4} tone="warning" detail="Runs out Thu" />
  <Meter label="Session (5h)" value={0.3} marker={0.55} detail="Lasts until reset" />
  <Meter variant="line" label="Opus weekly" value={0.7} marker={0.35} tone="error" />
</div>

# Ring

A glyph for toolbars: the label is its accessible name only.

<div className="flex items-center gap-3">
  <Meter variant="ring" label="Context" value={0.3} />
  <Meter variant="ring" label="Context" value={0.78} />
  <Meter variant="ring" label="Context" value={0.94} />
</div>

# Line

The bare bar for tight rows, such as a sidebar: stack two for a short and a long window.

<div className="flex w-40 flex-col gap-1">
  <Meter variant="line" label="Session (5h)" value={0.42} />
  <Meter variant="line" label="Weekly" value={0.81} />
</div>

# Compact

In a compact region the label steps down and the ring shrinks.

<SizeProvider size="compact">
  <div className="flex w-64 items-center gap-3">
    <Meter className="flex-1" label="Session" value={0.42} />
    <Meter variant="ring" label="Context" value={0.6} />
  </div>
</SizeProvider>
