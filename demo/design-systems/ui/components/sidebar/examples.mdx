# Resize, collapse and open on hover

Drag the rail on the inner edge to resize, click it or press `[` to collapse. With `openOnHover`, collapsing leaves the sidebar on hover: it opens when the pointer reaches the edge or the trigger beside it, and closes when it leaves. The trigger's dashed glyph shows the mode; press it to keep the sidebar open again.

<div className="flex h-[480px] w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background">
  <Sidebar.Provider className="h-full min-h-0" persist={false} mobileBreakpoint={0} openOnHover>
    <Sidebar variant="inset" className="h-full" defaultValue="Summarise the Q3 board deck">
      <Sidebar.Content>
        <Sidebar.Group>
          <Sidebar.GroupLabel>Threads</Sidebar.GroupLabel>

            {[

              ['Debug the eval harness timeout', 'unread', '9'],
            ].map(([label, status, badge]) => (
              <Sidebar.MenuItem key={label}>
                <Sidebar.MenuButton status={status} value={label}>{label}</Sidebar.MenuButton>
