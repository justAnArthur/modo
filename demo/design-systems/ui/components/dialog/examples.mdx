# Basic

A trigger, a header, and a footer whose Cancel is a `Dialog.Close`. The ✕
in the corner comes with `Dialog.Content`.

<Dialog>
  <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="tertiary">Open dialog</Button>} />
  <Dialog.Content>
    <Dialog.Header>
      <Dialog.Title>Create teamspace</Dialog.Title>
      <Dialog.Description>
        Add a new teamspace to organize your projects and collaborate with your team.
      </Dialog.Description>
    </Dialog.Header>
    <Dialog.Footer>
      <Dialog.Close render={<Button variant="ghost">Cancel</Button>} />
      <Button>Create</Button>
    </Dialog.Footer>
  </Dialog.Content>
</Dialog>

# Sizes

3 widths: `sm` for a confirmation, `lg` for a short form, `xl` for a
layout of its own. Each is one notch narrower inside a compact region.

<div className="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2">
  {[
    { size: 'sm', label: 'Small', copy: '400px: a confirmation, 1 field, a short message.' },
    { size: 'lg', label: 'Large', copy: '540px: a short form, or a longer confirmation.' },
    { size: 'xl', label: 'Extra large', copy: '880px: 2 columns, a table, or a sidebar beside a panel.' },
  ].map(({ size, label, copy }) => (
    <Dialog key={label}>
      <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">{label}</Button>} />
      <Dialog.Content size={size}>
        <Dialog.Header>
          <Dialog.Title>{label} dialog</Dialog.Title>
          <Dialog.Description>{copy}</Dialog.Description>
        </Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Footer>
          <Dialog.Close render={<Button variant="ghost">Close</Button>} />
        </Dialog.Footer>
      </Dialog.Content>
    </Dialog>
  ))}
</div>

# Surfaces inside a dialog

Not an FF docs section — a modo addition standing in for FF's "With a
sidebar" example, which this port omits along with Sidebar. The dialog
settles at `substrate + 4` and re-provides that level, so the Select's
popover lifts 2 more from there instead of painting the dialog's own
background. Nothing is passed between them.

<Dialog>
  <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Invite people</Button>} />
  <Dialog.Content>
    <Dialog.Header>
      <Dialog.Title>Invite to your workspace</Dialog.Title>
      <Dialog.Description>
        The panel sits 4 levels above the page; the role menu lifts 2 more from there.
      </Dialog.Description>
    </Dialog.Header>
    <Select defaultValue="member">
      <Select.Trigger placeholder="Select role" />
      <Select.Content>
        <Select.Item index={0} value="owner">Workspace owner</Select.Item>
        <Select.Item index={1} value="member">Member</Select.Item>
        <Select.Item index={2} value="restricted">Restricted member</Select.Item>
      </Select.Content>
    </Select>
    <Dialog.Footer>
      <Dialog.Close render={<Button variant="ghost">Cancel</Button>} />
      <Button>Send invites</Button>
    </Dialog.Footer>
  </Dialog.Content>
</Dialog>

# Morph sources

`from` picks where the panel grows from: the pressed trigger (the default), the press point, its own center, or a side of the window. `effect` switches the goo neck for a plain morph, a slide or a fade.

<div className="flex flex-wrap items-center justify-center gap-3">
  <Dialog>
    <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From the trigger</Button>} />
    <Dialog.Content>
      <Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Title>From the trigger</Dialog.Title>
        <Dialog.Description>The surface oozes out of the button and travels to the center.</Dialog.Description>
      </Dialog.Header>
    </Dialog.Content>
  </Dialog>
  <Dialog>
    <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From the pointer</Button>} />
    <Dialog.Content from="pointer" effect="morph">
      <Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Title>From the pointer</Dialog.Title>
        <Dialog.Description>A circle grows from where you pressed.</Dialog.Description>
      </Dialog.Header>
    </Dialog.Content>
  </Dialog>
  <Dialog>
    <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From its center</Button>} />
    <Dialog.Content from="center" effect="morph">
      <Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Title>From its center</Dialog.Title>
        <Dialog.Description>The panel unfolds in place toward every edge.</Dialog.Description>
      </Dialog.Header>
    </Dialog.Content>
  </Dialog>
  <Dialog>
    <Dialog.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From the bottom</Button>} />
    <Dialog.Content from="bottom" effect="slide">
      <Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Title>From the bottom</Dialog.Title>
        <Dialog.Description>The finished panel slides up from the edge of the window.</Dialog.Description>
      </Dialog.Header>
    </Dialog.Content>
  </Dialog>
</div>

# From a card

The trigger can be any element. `Morph.Part` pairs the card's cover and title with their twins in the panel, so they fly into the dialog and back.

<Dialog>
  <Dialog.Trigger className="flex w-56 flex-col gap-3 rounded-xl bg-surface-2 p-3 text-left shadow-surface-2 outline-none focus-visible:ring-1 focus-visible:ring-focus-ring">
    <Morph.Part id="cover" className="h-28 w-full rounded-lg bg-brand/20" />
    <Morph.Part id="title" className="text-subtitle weight-semibold text-foreground">Liquid glass</Morph.Part>
  </Dialog.Trigger>
  <Dialog.Content size="lg" effect="morph" hideSource>
    <div className="flex flex-col gap-4">
      <Morph.Part id="cover" className="h-48 w-full rounded-lg bg-brand/20" />
      <Morph.Part id="title" className="text-title weight-bold text-foreground">Liquid glass</Morph.Part>
      <Dialog.Description>The card grows into the dialog: its cover and title travel with it, and the rest of the panel is revealed around them.</Dialog.Description>
    </div>
  </Dialog.Content>
</Dialog>
