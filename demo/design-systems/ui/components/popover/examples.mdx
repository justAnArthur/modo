import Popover from './index'
import Button from '../button'
import InputGroup from '../input-group'

# Edit profile

The panel oozes out of the trigger through a liquid neck, then lets go. Escape or a press outside closes it back into the button.

<Popover>
  <Popover.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Edit profile</Button>} />
  <Popover.Content className="w-72 gap-3">
    <div className="flex flex-col gap-1">
      <Popover.Title>Edit profile</Popover.Title>
      <Popover.Description>Update how others see you.</Popover.Description>
    </div>
    <InputGroup>
      <InputGroup.Field index={0} label="Name" placeholder="Ada Lovelace" />
      <InputGroup.Field index={1} label="Username" placeholder="@ada" />
    </InputGroup>
    <div className="flex justify-end">
      <Popover.Close render={<Button size="compact">Save</Button>} />
    </div>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>

# Hover

With `openOnHover` the trigger opens it on hover too; a tap still toggles it on touch.

<Popover>
  <Popover.Trigger openOnHover delay={100} closeDelay={120} render={<Button variant="tertiary">Hover me</Button>} />
  <Popover.Content>
    <Popover.Title>Liquid preview</Popover.Title>
    <Popover.Description>Move away and it melts back into the button.</Popover.Description>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>

# Sides

# Morph

`effect="morph"` with `hideSource`: the trigger itself grows into the panel and shrinks back, one continuous surface.

<Popover>
  <Popover.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Add note</Button>} />
  <Popover.Content effect="morph" hideSource sideOffset={-36} className="w-72 gap-3">
    <Popover.Title>Add note</Popover.Title>
    <InputGroup>
      <InputGroup.Field index={0} label="Note" placeholder="Write something…" />
    </InputGroup>
    <div className="flex justify-end">
      <Popover.Close render={<Button size="compact">Done</Button>} />
    </div>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>

# Sources

`from` changes where the surface starts: the press point grows a circle under the cursor, `center` unfolds the panel in place.

<div className="flex flex-wrap items-center justify-center gap-3">
  <Popover>
    <Popover.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From the pointer</Button>} />
    <Popover.Content from="pointer">
      <Popover.Description>A circle from where you pressed.</Popover.Description>
    </Popover.Content>
  </Popover>
  <Popover>
    <Popover.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From its center</Button>} />
    <Popover.Content from="center" effect="morph">
      <Popover.Description>Unfolds from its own center.</Popover.Description>
    </Popover.Content>
  </Popover>
</div>
