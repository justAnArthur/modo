# Avatar

The first letter on an ink tile. Switch the shape in the Theme panel: the corners follow it.

<div className="flex items-center gap-3">
  <Avatar name="jobiflow" />
  <Avatar name="Work" />
  <Avatar name="zcode" tone="accent" />
</div>

# Tones

Ink and accent tell two kinds apart, such as your own accounts and a connected provider.

<div className="flex items-center gap-3">
  <Avatar name="Anthropic" tone="ink" />
  <Avatar name="OpenRouter" tone="accent" />
</div>

# Sizes

20px (`md`) for rows and triggers, 16px (`sm`) for dense lists.

<div className="flex items-center gap-3">
  <Avatar name="Ada" size="md" />
  <Avatar name="Ada" size="sm" />
  <Avatar name="Ada" size="md" tone="accent" />
  <Avatar name="Ada" size="sm" tone="accent" />
</div>

# Group

`Avatar.Group` overlaps a stack, each avatar cut out of the one beneath by a ring in the page color.

<Avatar.Group>
  <Avatar name="Ada" />
  <Avatar name="Grace" tone="accent" />
  <Avatar name="Linus" />
  <Avatar name="Margaret" tone="accent" />
</Avatar.Group>
