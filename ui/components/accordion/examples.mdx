# Basics

## Standalone

A single collapsible item with its own hover state.

<Accordion type="single" collapsible defaultValue="item-1">
  <Accordion.Item value="item-1">
    <Accordion.Trigger>What is this component?</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      A collapsible accordion with animated expand/collapse and spring-animated chevron.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion>

## Morph

Each panel drips out of the row where it was pressed (its middle, from the keyboard) and widens into the item, its content revealed by the same shape; closing folds it back into the row. The rows below ride its bottom edge.

  <Accordion.Item value="shipping" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>When does my order ship?</Accordion.Trigger>

      Orders placed before noon leave the same day. You get a tracking

# Variants

## Row highlight

With `highlight="trigger"` an expanded item holds no tint — the fill
scopes to the row and waits for hover. Suits dense panels, where a block
per open item reads as a second layer.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible defaultValue="item-1" highlight="trigger">
  <Accordion.Item value="item-1" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Expanded, but not tinted</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The panel reads as part of the page rather than a filled block.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="item-2" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Hover any row</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The fill follows your cursor instead of marking a state.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="item-3" index={2}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Compare with the sections above</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Those hold a block across the row and its panel while open.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>

## Without the chevron

`icon={false}` drops the glyph at the row's end, for a row whose own content says it opens. A component passed as `icon` replaces the chevron and turns a quarter turn on open, so give it a right-pointing glyph.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible defaultValue="plain">
  <Accordion.Item value="plain" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger icon={false}>No chevron</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The label takes the whole row; hover and the open tint still say what it does.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="default" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Default chevron</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The same row with the icon context's chevron.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="custom" index={2}>
    <Accordion.Trigger icon={ArrowRight}>Custom glyph</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      An arrow that points down once the item is open.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>

# Composition

## Rich headers

By default the trigger renders its label twice: a hidden semibold copy reserves the width, so the label gets heavier on open without moving. A header made of components would be duplicated in the DOM, so `weightShift={false}` renders it once and leaves the weight to its content. `contentClassName` is merged over the inner row's inset, padding and muted body text: the first panel below turns its text to full strength, the second takes a wider inset and the caption size. The label lives inside the trigger `<button>`, so build it from phrasing content (spans, as below), never a `div`-based header. Text that fixes its own colour stays that colour when the item opens: the chevron, the highlight and the open tint are what signal the state.

<Accordion.Group type="multiple" defaultValue={['one']}>
  <Accordion.Item value="one" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger weightShift={false}>
      <span className="flex flex-col">
        <span className="text-subtitle text-foreground">Pro plan</span>
        <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">Billed yearly, cancel any time</span>
      </span>
    </Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content contentClassName="text-foreground">
      Unlimited projects and priority support. The header above is in the DOM once.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="two" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger weightShift={false} icon={false}>
      <span className="flex flex-col">
        <span className="text-subtitle text-foreground">Team plan</span>
        <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">Per seat, no chevron</span>
      </span>
    </Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content contentClassName="px-6 text-caption">
      Shared workspaces and roles.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>

## Your own trigger

`asChild` (or `render`) makes your element the trigger, so a Button, a link-styled control or anything else can open the item. It is rendered as it is: the row's label copy, the chevron and the row padding are not added, so the element brings its own content and look. Base UI still wires `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `data-panel-open` and the keys onto it, so style the open state with `data-[panel-open]`.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible defaultValue="one">
  <Accordion.Item value="one" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger asChild>
      <Button variant="ghost" className="w-full justify-start">Billing</Button>
    </Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Invoices, payment methods and receipts.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="two" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger render={<Button variant="ghost" className="w-full justify-start" />}>
      Security
    </Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Passwords, sessions and two-factor.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>
