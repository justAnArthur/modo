import Accordion from './index'

# Standalone

A single collapsible item with its own hover state.

<Accordion type="single" collapsible defaultValue="item-1">
  <Accordion.Item value="item-1">
    <Accordion.Trigger>What is this component?</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      A collapsible accordion with animated expand/collapse and spring-animated chevron.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion>

# Row highlight

With `highlight="trigger"` an expanded item holds no tint — the fill
scopes to the row and waits for hover. Suits dense panels, where a block
per open item reads as a second layer.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible defaultValue="item-1" highlight="trigger">
  <Accordion.Item value="item-1" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Expanded, but not tinted</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The panel reads as part of the page rather than a filled block.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="item-2" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Hover any row</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      The fill follows your cursor instead of marking a state.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="item-3" index={2}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Compare with the sections above</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Those hold a block across the row and its panel while open.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>

# Morph

Each panel drips out of the row where it was pressed (its middle, from the keyboard) and widens into the item, its content revealed by the same shape; closing folds it back into the row. The rows below ride its bottom edge.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible defaultValue="shipping">
  <Accordion.Item value="shipping" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>When does my order ship?</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Orders placed before noon leave the same day. You get a tracking
      link by email as soon as the parcel is scanned.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="returns" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Can I return it?</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Anything unused comes back free within 30 days. Start a return from
      your order page and print the label.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="warranty" index={2}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>What does the warranty cover?</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content>
      Two years on parts and labour. Accidental damage is covered once,
      on top of that.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>

# Without the goo

`effect="morph"` grows the same shape with no liquid neck to its row; `tier="moderate"` runs the goo on the quicker, critically damped spring.

<Accordion.Group type="single" collapsible>
  <Accordion.Item value="morph" index={0}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Plain morph</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content effect="morph">
      The tint grows from the press point into the item, with no neck.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="moderate" index={1}>
    <Accordion.Trigger>Moderate tier</Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content tier="moderate">
      The goo again, on the moderate spring.
    </Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Group>
