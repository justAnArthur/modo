# System

The CPU load as the hero with its trend, the facts under it, the busiest processes beside it.

<WidgetIsland tab="system">
  <SystemStats samples={READINGS} battery={BATTERY} disk={DISK} uptime={UPTIME} processes={PROCESSES} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Memory pressure

When memory is what's tight, `hero="memory"` makes its pressure the figure; the line takes the warning hue.

<WidgetIsland tab="system">
  <SystemStats samples={PRESSURE_READINGS} hero="memory" battery={CHARGING} disk={DISK} uptime={UPTIME} processes={PROCESSES} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Without processes

No `processes`, no aside: the hero takes the width.

<WidgetIsland tab="system">
  <SystemStats samples={READINGS} disk={DISK} uptime={UPTIME} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Measuring

Before the first reading the figures are dashes over the Sparkline's baseline; the battery and the disk need no second reading.

<WidgetIsland tab="system">

</WidgetIsland>

# Compact

`SystemStats.Leading` and `SystemStats.Trailing`: the CPU glyph and its load in the wings.

<WingsIsland leading={<SystemStats.Leading />} trailing={<SystemStats.Trailing sample={READINGS[READINGS.length - 1]} />} />

# Minimal

`SystemStats.Minimal`, the load as a ring in the split island's bubble, music in the wings.

<WingsIsland
  leading={<NowPlaying.Leading track={PLAYING} />}
  trailing={<NowPlaying.Trailing track={PLAYING} />}
  minimal={<SystemStats.Minimal sample={READINGS[READINGS.length - 1]} />}
/>

# Tall

`layout="tall"` at the island's tall snap: CPU and memory pressure side by side with their trends, the network's in and out as one graph beside the battery and the disk, each headed by its glyph, and the top three processes as a table.

<WidgetIsland tab="system" tall>
  <SystemStats samples={READINGS} battery={BATTERY} disk={DISK} uptime={UPTIME} processes={PROCESSES} layout="tall" />
