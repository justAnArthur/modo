import Switch from './index'

# Basic

A labelled switch. Uncontrolled here — it keeps its own state; pass
`checked` and `onToggle` to control it.

<Switch label="Notifications" />

# Disabled

`disabled` dims the row and ignores pointer and keyboard input.

<Switch label="Disabled option" disabled />

# Compact

`size="compact"` shrinks the track, thumb and label one step down the
size ladder. `defaultChecked` starts it on.

<div className="flex flex-col">
  <Switch size="compact" label="Auto-save" defaultChecked />
  <Switch size="compact" label="Show hidden files" />
</div>

# Description

`description` adds a muted line under the label, and `trackSide="end"` pushes the track to the far edge, as in a settings row.

<Switch
  className="w-80 max-w-full"
  trackSide="end"
  label="Auto-update"
  description="Install updates in the background and restart when idle"
/>

# Group

`Switch.Group` frames switches as a settings list: each one becomes a row with its track at the far edge. Here beside a label column, as on a settings page; on a narrow page the label stacks above it.

<div className="@container w-full max-w-3xl">
  <div className="grid gap-3 @xl:grid-cols-[10rem_1fr] @xl:gap-6">
    <p id="automation" className="text-body weight-medium text-foreground [text-box:trim-both_cap_alphabetic] @xl:pt-4">
      Automation
    </p>
    <Switch.Group aria-labelledby="automation">
      <Switch
        label="Remove Task OUT with old contracts"
        description="When an old contract is removed automatically, its Task OUT is removed too"
      />
      <Switch
        label="Block billing until damage is inspected"
        description="A contract can't be marked as billed while a damage inspection is required"
      />
    </Switch.Group>
  </div>
</div>

# Group states

Every switch in a group keeps its own state; a disabled row gets no highlight. Rows work without a description too.

<Switch.Group aria-label="Notifications" className="w-96 max-w-full">
  <Switch label="Email" defaultChecked />
  <Switch label="Push notifications" />
  <Switch label="SMS" description="Not available in your region" disabled />
</Switch.Group>

# Compact group

`size` on the group pins every row to one step of the size ladder.

<Switch.Group size="compact" aria-label="Editor" className="w-80 max-w-full">
  <Switch label="Auto-save" description="Save after every change" defaultChecked />
  <Switch label="Format on save" />
  <Switch label="Show hidden files" />
</Switch.Group>
