import NowPlaying from './index'

# Player

The artwork as the hero's picture with a glow of its tint, the title, artist and album, the scrubber ticking on by itself, the transport, the app and the device it plays on, and shuffle, repeat and the heart.

<WidgetIsland tab="now-playing">
  <NowPlaying track={PLAYING} defaultShuffle={false} defaultRepeat="all" defaultLiked />
</WidgetIsland>

# Paused, no extras

Without shuffle, repeat or like values or handlers, their buttons stay out; paused, the bars settle.

<WidgetIsland tab="now-playing">
  <NowPlaying track={{ ...PLAYING, output: undefined }} defaultPlaying={false} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Without artwork

`artwork: null` says the track has none: the note placeholder takes the artwork's place. Left out, the artwork is still loading, and the last picture stays up.

<WidgetIsland tab="now-playing">
  <NowPlaying track={{ ...TRACK, artwork: null }} defaultPlaying={false} />
</WidgetIsland>

# A stream

A `duration` of `0` hides the scrubber.

  />
</WidgetIsland>

# Compact

`NowPlaying.Leading` and `NowPlaying.Trailing` in the wings: the artwork, and the bars in its tint, settling low while paused.

<div className="flex w-full flex-col gap-3">

  <WingsIsland leading={<NowPlaying.Leading track={PLAYING} />} trailing={<NowPlaying.Trailing track={PLAYING} playing={false} />} />
</div>
