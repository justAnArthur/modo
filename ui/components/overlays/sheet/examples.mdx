import Sheet from './index'
import Button from '../../button'

# Bottom sheet

The sheet oozes up out of the bottom edge. Swipe it back down, press outside or hit Escape to send it back.

<Sheet>
  <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Open sheet</Button>} />
  <Sheet.Content>
    <Sheet.Header>
      <Sheet.Title>Share project</Sheet.Title>
      <Sheet.Description>Anyone with the link can view it.</Sheet.Description>
    </Sheet.Header>
    <Sheet.Footer>
      <Sheet.Close render={<Button variant="ghost">Cancel</Button>} />
      <Sheet.Close render={<Button>Copy link</Button>} />
    </Sheet.Footer>
  </Sheet.Content>
</Sheet>

# Sides

`side` attaches the sheet to any edge; it grows from that edge and is swiped back toward it.

<div className="flex flex-wrap items-center justify-center gap-3">
  <Sheet side="right">
    <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Right</Button>} />
    <Sheet.Content>
      <Sheet.Title>Filters</Sheet.Title>
      <Sheet.Description>A side sheet takes the full height and caps its width.</Sheet.Description>
    </Sheet.Content>
  </Sheet>
  <Sheet side="left">
    <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Left</Button>} />
    <Sheet.Content>
      <Sheet.Title>Navigation</Sheet.Title>
      <Sheet.Description>Swipe left to dismiss.</Sheet.Description>
    </Sheet.Content>
  </Sheet>
  <Sheet side="top">
    <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Top</Button>} />
    <Sheet.Content>
      <Sheet.Title>Announcement</Sheet.Title>
      <Sheet.Description>Swipe up to dismiss.</Sheet.Description>
    </Sheet.Content>
  </Sheet>
</div>

# From the trigger

`from="trigger"` grows the sheet out of the button instead of the edge; once open it behaves like any sheet.

<Sheet>
  <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">From the button</Button>} />
  <Sheet.Content from="trigger">
    <Sheet.Title>From the button</Sheet.Title>
    <Sheet.Description>The surface melts out of the trigger and settles at the bottom edge.</Sheet.Description>
  </Sheet.Content>
</Sheet>

# Snap points

A top or bottom sheet can rest at `snapPoints`: here 40% of the window, then nearly all of it. Drag the handle between them.

<Sheet snapPoints={[0.4, 0.9]}>
  <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Open with snap points</Button>} />
  <Sheet.Content className="min-h-[90dvh]">
    <Sheet.Title>Two heights</Sheet.Title>
    <Sheet.Description>Swipe up to expand, down to fold back or dismiss.</Sheet.Description>
  </Sheet.Content>
</Sheet>

# Indent

Wrap the page in `Sheet.Indent` under a `Sheet.Provider` and it eases back while a sheet is open.

<Sheet.Provider>
  <Sheet.Indent className="flex w-full flex-col items-center gap-3 rounded-xl bg-surface-2 p-8">
    <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">The page behind the sheet</span>
    <Sheet>
      <Sheet.Trigger render={<Button variant="secondary">Open sheet</Button>} />
      <Sheet.Content>
        <Sheet.Title>On top of the page</Sheet.Title>
        <Sheet.Description>The page scales back a little while this is open.</Sheet.Description>
      </Sheet.Content>
    </Sheet>
  </Sheet.Indent>
</Sheet.Provider>
