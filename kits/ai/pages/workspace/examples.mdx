# Main window

A 1280 × 800 window, scaled to the column, on DesktopShell with no title bar. Press ⌘K or click the sidebar's search to switch sessions, run a command or open a pane. A session mid-turn: the task list pinned under it, the composer streaming, the changes beside it. Pick another session, switch panes, open the chapter list.

</ScreenFrame>

# The claude process crashed

When a profile's claude exits mid-turn, a banner over the transcript shows its last stderr with a restart; the failure-states block has the other kinds (reconnecting, rate-limited, offline, auth expired). The bell in the title bar is the inbox; the search beside it looks through every session's transcript; ⌘/ shows the shortcuts. Double-click the title to rename the session.
