# Tasks

Background agents, shells and child sessions: stop a running one, retry one that failed or stopped. Stop and Retry show with their callbacks.

<div className="w-full max-w-sm overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-background p-2">
  <SizeProvider size="compact">
    <TasksPane tasks={PANE_TASKS} onStop={() => {}} onRetry={() => {}} />
  </SizeProvider>
</div>
