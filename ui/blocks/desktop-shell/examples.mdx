# macOS window

The title bar starts after the traffic lights (drawn here with `showControls`; a native window draws its own). Fold the sidebar with its toggle or `[`, the side pane with `]`. Drag the sidebar's rail, or the gutter between the card and the pane, to resize; double-click the gutter to reset it. The window is 960px wide: scroll it sideways in a narrow column.

# Folded sidebar

The sidebar starts folded. Hover the window's left edge or the sidebar toggle and it peeks: it slides in over the main card, which doesn't move, and folds back a beat after the pointer leaves; focus inside keeps it open. Click the toggle to pin it. The traffic lights, the toggle and the title share one centre line; `peek={false}` turns the peek off.

<div className="w-full overflow-x-auto">
<div className="h-[560px] w-[960px] overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border">
  <DesktopShell
    showControls
    titleBar="card"
    defaultLeftOpen={false}
