  <Message role="user">Which overlays skip the morph layer?</Message>
  <Message role="assistant">
    <p className="m-0">None of them. Every overlay grows through <code>useMorph</code>:</p>
    <ul className="m-0">
      <li><strong>Popover</strong>, Menu and Select morph out of their trigger.</li>
      <li><strong>Dialog</strong> morphs out of the button that opened it.</li>

  </Message>
</div>
