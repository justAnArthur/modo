# Clock

The time as the hero, the date above it and the time zone under it, the Pomodoro waiting with its lengths; beside it the world clocks, each with how far it is, day or night, and Tomorrow when its date differs.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <Clock zones={ZONES} now={CLOCK_NOW} />
</WidgetIsland>

# 24 hours, with seconds

`hour12={false}` and `seconds`: the seconds tick muted, on each second's edge.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <Clock zones={ZONES} hour12={false} seconds pomodoro={{ focus: 50, shortBreak: 10, longBreak: 30, rounds: 3 }} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Focus

A run takes the widget over: the ring in the phase's hue, the time left, the round, when it ends and what's next, and the next phases with their start times. Pause, skip, +1 min or reset it.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <FocusClock />
</WidgetIsland>

# A break, paused

A paused phase keeps the time it had left and says so.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <PausedBreak />
</WidgetIsland>

# Ready to start

`Start focus` begins the first round; at a phase's end the next one waits here, paused at its whole length.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <Clock zones={ZONES} now={CLOCK_NOW} defaultValue={{ phase: 'long-break', round: 4, total: 15 * 60_000, remaining: 15 * 60_000 }} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Clock only

`pomodoro={false}` leaves the Pomodoro out.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock">
  <Clock zones={ZONES.slice(0, 2)} now={CLOCK_NOW} pomodoro={false} />
</WidgetIsland>

# Compact

`Clock.Leading` and `Clock.Trailing` in the wings while a phase runs: the ring and the time left. Without a run they show nothing.

<ClockWings />

# Tall

`layout="tall"` at the island's tall snap: the time large, every world clock as a column, the Pomodoro's whole cycle as a strip over its three lengths, and today's finished rounds (`completedToday`).

<WidgetIsland tab="clock" tall>
  <Clock zones={ZONES} now={CLOCK_NOW} completedToday={3} layout="tall" />
</WidgetIsland>

# Tall, in focus

A run at the tall snap: the ring large beside the time left, then every phase of the cycle, done, now and when each starts.

<WidgetIsland tab="clock" tall>
  <FocusClock completedToday={5} layout="tall" />
