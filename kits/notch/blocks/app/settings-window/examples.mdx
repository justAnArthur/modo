# Permissions

Allowed, Not asked, Off and Needs reset, each with its one action; a spinner while one is read. The sidebar counts the ones that need you.

<MockWindow title="Settings">
  <SettingsWindow name="Just Notch" defaultValue="permissions" plugins={PLUGINS} permissions={PERMISSIONS} about={ABOUT} />
</MockWindow>

# About

`aboutHeader` sets the app's icon, name, version and © over the `about` rows; without it the page is titled About and holds the rows alone.

<MockWindow title="Settings">
  <SettingsWindow name="Just Notch" defaultValue="about" plugins={PLUGINS} permissions={PERMISSIONS} aboutHeader={ABOUT_HEADER} about={ABOUT_DETAILS} />
</MockWindow>

# A plugin's settings

Reached from its row in Plugins, which stays lit; a Plugins button leads back. Grouped rows: a label, what it does, the control at the end.
