# Becoming the panel

With `hideSource`, the trigger disappears while the panel is open, so it reads as turning into it. With `effect="morph"` too, the panel opens in place, over its trigger: press the panel to close it.

<div className="flex w-full flex-wrap items-start justify-center gap-x-6 gap-y-48 pb-40">
  <Morph hideSource label="Goo">
    <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">The trigger melts into the panel.</span>
  </Morph>
  <Morph hideSource effect="morph" label="Morph">
    <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">The trigger grows into the panel.</span>
  </Morph>
</div>

# Two speeds

Goo always runs on `spring.goo`, so the neck has time to stretch; a plain morph takes a tier: `moderate` for small panels, `slow` by default.

<div className="flex w-full flex-wrap items-start justify-center gap-x-6 gap-y-48 pb-40">
  <Morph effect="morph" tier="moderate" label="Moderate">
    <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">spring.moderate</span>
  </Morph>
  <Morph effect="morph" tier="slow" label="Slow">
    <span className="text-caption text-muted-foreground">spring.slow</span>
  </Morph>
</div>
